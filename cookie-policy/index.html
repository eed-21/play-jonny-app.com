What are cookies?

Cookies represent small text files that websites store on user devices to remember information about previous visits and user preferences. These digital markers contain alphanumeric strings that help play jonny platforms recognize returning users and maintain session continuity across multiple gaming sessions. Modern web browsers automatically manage cookie storage and retrieval processes, though users retain control over cookie acceptance and deletion through browser settings. Different cookie types serve various purposes, from maintaining login sessions to tracking user preferences for personalized gaming experiences. First-party cookies originate directly from the gaming platform itself, while third-party cookies may come from external service providers that support platform functionality. Session cookies expire when users close their browsers, whereas persistent cookies remain on devices for predetermined periods to maintain user preferences across multiple visits. Understanding these fundamental concepts helps players navigate privacy settings more effectively.
